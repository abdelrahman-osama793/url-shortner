About Shortlist

Shortlist turns long web addresses into short links you can share anywhere. Anyone can make one in seconds, no account needed. With an account you keep control afterwards: choose a memorable alias, change where a link points, pause it, set an expiry date and see how often it's clicked.

What we care about

  • Changes apply straight away. Short links aren't cached, so the next click after an edit or a pause follows it.
  • Honest numbers. We count clicks, not "unique visitors", and say when counts can include repeat visits or bots.
  • Light on visitor data. A click keeps only its time, approximate country, device type and referring site, and individual clicks are deleted after 30 days.
  • Safe links for everyone. Anyone can report a harmful link. Administrators review every report, and every action they take is recorded with a reason.
