Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

This policy explains what Shortlist stores, why, for how long, and what you can do about it. Shortlist is run by {{ operator.name }} ("we"), based in {{ operator.jurisdiction }}. Questions go to {{ operator.email }}.

What we store and why

Your account

  • Your email address, whether you've confirmed it, and when the account was created.
  • If you use a password: a one-way Argon2id hash of it. We never store or see the password itself.
  • If you sign in with Google: the identifier Google gives your Google account, and the email address Google confirms.
  • Your account's role (user or administrator) and status (active or suspended).

We need these to run your account and keep it secure.

Your links

For each link: the destination address, its short code or alias, any expiry date you set, whether it's paused, and when it was created and last changed. Links made without an account are kept the same way but have no owner and stop working after 7 days.

Clicks on links

When someone opens a short link we record:

  • the time and which link;
  • an approximate country (currently not determined, so it shows as "Unknown");
  • a device type (desktop, mobile, tablet, bot or unknown) worked out from the browser's description of itself;
  • the referring website's domain name only, not the full address;
  • whether the click looks automated.

We don't store the visitor's IP address, full browser description or full referring address with a click. Individual clicks are deleted after 30 days. Daily totals are kept for 12 months, and a lifetime total for as long as the link exists. Only the link's owner can see its numbers.

Sign-in sessions

When you use the site we set one cookie holding a random session identifier. On our side the session records who is signed in and when it started. Sessions end after 24 hours without use, or 7 days at most (administrators: 1 hour and 12 hours). See the Cookie Policy.

Preventing abuse

To limit how fast links can be created and accounts used, we count requests per network address and per email address. The counters store a keyed hash of the address, never the address itself, and disappear when their time window ends (at most one day).

Reports

If you report a link we store the link, the category and any details you write. If you were signed in, the report is linked to your account. We don't ask guests for contact details.

Emails

We email you to confirm your address and to reset your password. Messages waiting to be sent are stored encrypted, and their contents are erased once sent or expired; a record that an email was sent remains. Emails are delivered through [email provider].

Server logs

Our servers log each request's path, result and duration to keep the service running. They don't log IP addresses, query strings or the contents of forms. Logs are kept for [log retention period].

Moderation records

When an administrator blocks a link or suspends an account, we keep a permanent record of who did it, to what, when and why. These records are needed to handle abuse and can't be changed or deleted, including after an account is deleted.

Who else handles data

  • Hosting and databases: [hosting provider], which stores the data above on our behalf.
  • Email delivery: [email provider].
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google or allow its sign-in button.

We don't sell personal data or use it for advertising.
