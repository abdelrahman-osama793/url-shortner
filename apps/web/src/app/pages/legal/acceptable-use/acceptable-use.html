Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Short links hide where they lead, so people have to be able to trust them. Don't use Shortlist to:

  • link to phishing pages or anything built to steal passwords, payment details or personal data;
  • spread malware, or link to downloads that harm devices;
  • send spam, or hide destinations to get around spam or safety filters;
  • link to content that is illegal where you or your audience are, including content that sexually exploits children;
  • harass, threaten or impersonate people or organisations;
  • infringe other people's copyright, trademarks or privacy;
  • overload the service, get around its limits or verification checks, or create links or accounts automatically beyond what the service allows.

What happens

Administrators review reports and can block a link or suspend the account behind it. A blocked link shows visitors a plain "no longer active" page, without revealing its destination. Every action is recorded with a reason. Serious illegal content may be reported to the authorities.

Reporting a link

Anyone can report a link, no account needed. We don't tell the link's owner who reported it. For anything else, contact {{ operator.email }}.
