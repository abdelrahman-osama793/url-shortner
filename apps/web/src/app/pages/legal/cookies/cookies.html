Cookie Policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

Shortlist uses very little browser storage. Necessary storage is always on, because the site can't work without it. Everything else is optional and used only if you allow it.

What we use

Name Type Purpose Kept for
__Host-session Necessary cookie Keeps you signed in and ties each form to your browser so it can't be forged from another site. Unreadable by page scripts and sent only over a secure connection to this site. Until the session ends: 24 hours without use or 7 days at most (administrators 1 and 12 hours; before signing in, 2 hours)
shortlist.consent Necessary, browser storage Remembers the choice you made here, so we don't ask on every page. Until you change it or clear this site's data
shortlist.theme Preferences, browser storage Remembers whether you chose light, dark or system appearance. Removed if you withdraw consent. Until you change it, withdraw consent or clear this site's data
Google sign-in Optional, set by Google Loads Google's sign-in button from accounts.google.com. Google sets its own cookies under Google's privacy policy. Without it you can still sign in with email and password. Set by Google

What we don't use

No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts other than Google sign-in when you allow it.

Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw consent at any time in Cookie settings. We then stop using that storage and delete what we stored ourselves. Cookies Google has already set can only be removed from your browser or your Google account settings.
