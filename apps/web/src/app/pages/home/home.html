What you can count on

Changes apply straight away

Short links aren't cached. Once an edit or a pause is saved, the very next click follows it.

Honest numbers

We count clicks, not "unique visitors". Repeat visits and bots can be included, and we say so.

Light on visitor data

A click keeps only its time, approximate country, device type and referring site. IP addresses aren't stored with it, and individual clicks are deleted after 30 days.

Harmful links get taken down

Anyone can report a link, no account needed. Administrators review reports, and every action they take is recorded with a reason.
